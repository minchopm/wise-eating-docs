## At a glance

| Capability | What the product documents | Boundary to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food composition | USDA FoodData Central-based entries with serving and per-100 g nutrient views. | Database values are reference measurements, not a laboratory analysis of your specific meal. |
| Missing data | The product distinguishes missing nutrient values from measured zero. | A missing value must not be counted as evidence that a nutrient is absent. |
| Planning and pantry | Food diary, quantities, expiry tracking, shopping lists and optional AI planning. | Verify portions, ingredients and allergens. Generated plans are suggestions, not medical prescriptions. |
